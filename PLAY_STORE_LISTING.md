```
Munal AI is your all-in-one AI-powered meeting companion that transforms how teams capture, organize, and act on meeting insights.

KEY FEATURES:

Smart Meeting Management
• Record and transcribe meetings with high-accuracy AI
• Automatic summaries with key decisions and context
• Extract and assign action items automatically
• Schedule and manage meetings in one place

AI Assistant
• Chat with AI to get instant answers about your meetings
• Generate reports, summaries, and follow-ups
• Smart search across all your meeting content
• Powered by GPT for intelligent responses

Document Hub
• Built-in PDF editor with annotations and drawing tools
• Electronic signatures for contracts and agreements
• File format converter — PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, EPUB, and more
• Batch file conversion support
• 6 pre-made document templates (NDA, contracts, invoices)
• Custom document templates for organizations

Workspaces
• Create shared workspaces for your teams
• Real-time collaboration on documents
• Organized file management with search

Enterprise Security
• Two-factor authentication (2FA) with TOTP or email OTP
• Role-based access control (Super Admin, Admin, Manager, User)
• Organization management with team hierarchies
• Comprehensive audit logging and compliance tracking
• Security compliance dashboard with health scoring

Additional Features
• Dark mode with beautiful UI
• Mobile-responsive design
• Push notifications to stay updated
• Form builder for custom workflows
• ICT support ticket tracking
• Announcement broadcasting

Munal AI helps teams save hours every week by eliminating manual note-taking, automating follow-ups, and keeping everyone aligned. Whether you're a startup team or an enterprise organization, Munal AI scales with your needs.

Download now and transform your meetings into actionable outcomes.
```
